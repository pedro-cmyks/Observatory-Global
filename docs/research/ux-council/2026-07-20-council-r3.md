## 2. THE NEW SURFACES VERDICT — is the dossier publishable now?

R2 closed on the dossier as **"draft yes, file no — one lane away."** The three new surfaces this round — **Workbench article-enrichment (F1 fetch + F2 AI-read/crossread + LEADS)**, the **sealed Brief daily edition**, and the **enriched dossier** — were built to close that lane. Verdict of the panel:

**Consensus: still "draft yes, file no." The enrichment closes the "can't read the body" half of the gap and does so honestly — but it does NOT close the corroboration/independence half, and it opened one new trust hole.**

**What genuinely landed (all grounded):**
- **AI-read quote-grounding is honest.** The read pass drops quoteless claims at parse (per spec); Inés confirmed `articles_read:2 articles_with_claims:2` with **3 quote-backed tensions in 0.45s** (vs R2's 23–26s SERP path); Poneperos confirmed cache-first, quote-grounded, 200 end-to-end. This is the real win — the body-reading lane works and doesn't fabricate.
- **Cross-language payoff is real** (per the enrichment ship note): full-text excerpts enter the synthesis under the same `[n]` receipt number, surfacing Thai/naval-drone detail absent from English headlines.

**What blocks "file":**
- **P0 — the sealed DAILY EDITION can lead with unattributed state propaganda.** [Marcos] The sealed edition's flagship "Civilian Casualties in Drone Attacks" (selection score 0.932) leads with citation `[1] = russian.rt.com` (RT — sanctioned state media) in Russian, **corroboration `null`**. A sealed "edition" that leads with uncorroborated state propaganda is not publishable by construction.
- **P1 — cross-read INFLATES corroboration (no independence check).** [Poneperos #1, Carolina F1, Inés F3] The AI cross-read labels any two agreeing quote-backed claims **"✓ corroboration"** with **zero shared-source / independence test** — Poneperos reproduced multiple "✓ corroboration" findings from a single-event pin set; Carolina found it produced **0 real corroborations between two articles reporting the same event and over-labeled everything "tension."** Corroboration that can't tell independent sourcing from syndication is not a publish warranty.
- **P1 — the corroboration verdict has ZERO clickable receipts.** [Tomás] `POST /corroborate` returns "corroborated by 16 sources" but **16/16 corroborating + 4/4 contradicting carry `url:null`** (hardcoded — `signal_embeddings` join doesn't carry `source_url`). On the exact surface built to deliver receipts, the analyst cannot click through to one of them.
- **Coverage ceiling:** cross-read only corroborates over sources that *extract* (~2 of 6 pinned here, ~50% fetch yield) and only within the pin set — pin 2, lose 1, and you get nothing.

**Net:** the enrichment is real and its quote-grounding is the most honest thing built this cycle. But "publishable" requires **independent corroboration with clickable receipts**, and all three of those failed: cross-read conflates syndication with corroboration, `/corroborate` verdicts are link-less, and the sealed edition will lead with state media it never corroborated. The dossier is still one lane away — and that lane is now precisely diagnosed: **independent, receipt-linked corroboration**, not body-reading.

## 4. VERDICT

**Is Atlas dependably honest now? Closer — and demonstrably so.** R2 read "dependably honest, not yet dependably there." This round, the three items that R2 could only call "fails honestly" — universe dark, deep-link raw id, seal banner — converted to **actual fixes, re-verified live by ≥5 personas each**, and the seal banner now self-labels its own inference ("schedule inference, not a live observation") — the honesty discipline is maturing into fixes, not just labels. **No R1/R2 P0 lies survive on a prominent surface silently.** The remaining dishonesty is either contained-and-labeled (N8 counts, N14 confidence) or engine-substrate that surfaces flag but can't heal (N11 subject-geo/label quality).

**Is the dossier publishable? Not yet — still "draft yes, file no," and the blocking lane is now precisely one:** **independent, receipt-linked corroboration.** The enrichment did the honest, hard thing — full-text body reading with quote-grounded claims that drop the unquoted — but corroboration regressed into a *new* dishonesty: cross-read stamps "✓ corroboration" on syndicated agreement with no independence check (R3-2), `/corroborate` verdicts carry no clickable URLs (R3-3), and a sealed edition will lead with uncorroborated Russian state media (R3-1). **The single highest-leverage next fix: make corroboration mean independent corroboration with clickable receipts** — add a shared-source/independence gate to cross-read, carry `source_url` through the corroborate join, and block a *sealed* edition from leading with an uncorroborated (or state-media) citation. That one lane closes both the "file no" gap and the R3-1 P0 at once.

## 4. THE MOVES

Round-1's three moves (stop the front page lying · complete the capture loop · make the dossier publishable) delivered their first two phases — this round measured it. The new list implies three successors:

### Move 1 — Stamp before you serve (close the warranty gap) → N1, N2, N15
The court's verdict becomes a *precondition of prominence*, not a nightly audit. (a) Lead/top-N slots require `label_status: entailed` — an unstamped thread cannot lead; it yields to the next stamped thread or serves a receipt-derived neutral label (the demotion path already built). (b) A `failed` verdict renders "LABEL UNDER REVIEW" on **every** surface serving that label — chip wiring becomes per-label, not per-list. (c) Court cadence ties to the serving snapshot (stamp at promotion), so the freshest news is the *first* stamped, not the last. (d) Extend the same principle to provenance metadata: LOCAL/COVERED-FROM chips must be entailed by the receipt's own outlet origin (`source_origin_country` exists — the chip logic contradicts it). One sentence for the whole move: **labels earned receipts in round 2; now every chip earns its receipt, before it ships.**

### Move 2 — Pipelines earn their banners (the dependability program) → N3, N4, N10 (+N5 rider)
Round-1 consensus #3 — honest failure ≠ acceptable failure — is now the top of the stack. (a) **Seal:** alert on failure, recompute "next attempt" honestly (never print a past time as a future promise), target seven consecutive sealed mornings as the exit criterion. (b) **Universe:** dark ≠ down — fix the empty-build path or pre-announce in-UI; an empty 200 after 46s is a lie of form even when the payload is honest. (c) **Corroboration:** the SERP/Brave key remains the highest leverage-per-effort line in the whole program (round 1 and round 2 agree); separately fix archive recall — the lane must find the BBC story that is already pinned in the same investigation; keep the structured `source_status` (it's good) and surface it *before* the click. **Rider:** freeze threads-list order while the pointer is over the panel (N5) — three of six personas mis-opened; no dependability claim survives a first click that lands wrong.

### Move 3 — Validate claims, not just numbers (prose/table contract v2) → N7, N8, N13
The validator that fixed −0.51 = −0.51 is checking equality; the remaining lies are *entailment* failures — "Spain shows positive sentiment" against a table with no Spain. Reuse the Label Court's machinery on generated prose: every directional/superlative claim in Editor's Analysis and the dossier must be entailed by a same-page table row, one sentiment scale per page (kill the −0.42/−10.0 dual-scale paragraph), and count-contract v2 reconciles the last diverging bases (header vs bottom bar, `country_count`, focus-header scope). This is also the gate that Diana's round-1 endgame (dossier voice on the front page) has been waiting behind — it ships when this validator holds.

*Not a move, still named:* N11 NER garbage stays on the P2.5 actor-quality track; N6 substrate healing (vs flagging) is engine-roadmap work (#224/#238) sequenced after the P0s — containment held this round and must keep holding.

**What converted.** This is the round the fixes stopped being labels: **N5 is dead after three broken cycles** — freeze-order held under live adversarial hover on three independent browser seats; **the front-door count pair reconciled to the digit for the first time in council history**, cross-confirmed at four different probe times; the court fold went **21/36 failed → 0 failed** with a working `court_withheld` chip on every unchecked console row; corroboration receipts went **16/16 null → clickable on every row** across six seats; the dossier walk finally re-ran clean and its honesty rails refused to invent a story from a hollow pin; and the four new dimensions establish that the mobile re-shape, the desktop focus spine, the relation receipts, and the L0→L3 ladder are all structurally real. **What regressed.** The disease changed phase: **R3's problem was missing labels; R4's problem is labels that lie.** The sealed edition stamps Syria's and China's state agencies `is_state_media: false, tier: "mainstream"` while its own prose calls SANA "Syrian state media" — an asserted falsehood where R3-1's fix left an honest null, filed independently by five seats (N18). **What the release still misses.** The R3 gap — *independent, receipt-linked corroboration* — is half-closed: receipts linked, independence still asserted rather than measured (ownership-blind, paraphrase-blind, event-blind). The new precise blocking set: **(1)** make the trust metadata as honest as the prose — one ~20-line state-domain port to `source_tiers.py`, tier fields on every receipt-bearing payload, and a court check that the label's own geography matches its receipts before it can lead a country door; **(2)** make independence measured — origin-tracing + ownership tier + event identity in the corroboration verdict; **(3)** make the window stamps true — the lifetime-as-24h detail mislabel and the future-timestamp clamp are both mechanical; **(4)** give mobile back its attention organ and its pin.

## 2. CONSENSUS

### Strongest — keep and protect (unanimous)
1. **The honesty architecture as a system**: "What Atlas is not," degraded banners, VERIFIED/BELOW_GATE tiers, "volume replay" relabeling, honest empty states, gap notes, downranking ledgers. Six for six called it the product's identity. *Protect it from dilution — and from becoming an excuse (see weakest #3).*
2. **Receipts everywhere, real and one click deep** — domain, language, date, clickable URL, frozen at pin time. Every persona used receipts to catch the product's own errors; that paradox is the design working.
3. **The dossier** — cited synthesis, WHAT WE DON'T KNOW, 5W+H readiness, typed relations ("actor overlap not causality"), named model + grounding, self-flagging of its own contaminated inputs. Verdict 6/6: *use internally today; publish, no.*
4. **The time scrubber** and per-thread Deep History with clickable per-day archive receipts.
5. **Under the Radar / coverage gaps** — raw/verified with numerator AND denominator; absence as a finding. Carolina: "I would open this tab every morning before the front page."
6. **Search as intent router** (Open the story / Go to country / Start investigation) and **focus propagation** re-scoping the whole console.
7. **Frozen pins + append-only trail** — "audit-grade investigation memory" (Tomas), "chain of custody" (Marcos).

### Weakest — fix first (unanimous or near)
1. **Label ≠ receipts** (6/6, every persona's #1). Systemic cluster contamination + mislabeling reaching the most prominent surfaces, while the engine's own 21%-confidence score is ignored by ranking.
2. **Numbers that don't reconcile** (5/6): 42/347/3,762 for one thread; -0.52 vs -0.1 on one page; "1 COUNTRIES" vs "across 3 countries"; header vs bottom-bar totals. Tomas: "trust the receipts, not the labels — and this dossier's receipts are solid while its labels and prose still need line-editing."
3. **Honest failure ≠ acceptable failure**: 2-day-stale Brief, dead corroboration lane, Universe endpoint down for two panelists, dead landing stats. The banners are correct; the pipelines behind them still have to work.
4. **Capture is incomplete**: evidence headlines — the atoms of an argument — cannot be pinned; pins fragment into silent duplicate investigations; ◆ targets are tiny and rows move under the cursor.
5. **Encoding hygiene**: raw HTML entities on at least five surfaces, worst in Cyrillic ("literal hex soup" — Diana).

## 5. HOW WE CONTINUE — the next three moves

The evidence forces one reading: **the honesty layer is built and believed; the substrate feeding it is what fails, and it fails most visibly exactly where the product is most prominent.** All six "one big ideas" are the same idea. Every persona's dossier verdict was "use yes / publish no," and all four professionals gave overlapping blocker lists: labels, corroboration, tiers, prose-vs-tables. So we sequence for those blockers, cheapest trust-per-line first.

### Move 1 — Stop the front page lying (surface work, days; plus a hygiene batch)
**Product-surface:** the confidence number already exists (21% vs 96–100%) — gate the Brief lead slot and share card on it; demote failures to receipt-derived labels or an "unassembled signals" tray (the Under-the-Radar pattern, already built and loved, reused). Fix the placeholder-title flash (regression of an already-shipped rule). Ship the one-day hygiene batch: `html.unescape` everywhere (known small fix), one sentiment scale, count qualifiers ("gated · in-window · lifetime") wherever a number prints, export toasts, the landing `$ countries` template, kill the jargon leaks (#219 tooltip, changed_10h in WHY, debug search chips).
**Why first:** this is the entire P0 tier minus the ops item, it needs no new engine capability, and per Ines's arithmetic every visible mismatch "costs more trust than ten honest empty states buy back." The Label Court entailment check (engine-side, #204 track) starts in parallel but does not gate this move — confidence gating alone removes the worst lies today.

### Move 2 — Complete the capture loop (product-surface, ~a week)
Receipt-level pinning (the council's #1 wish, five of six personas; Carolina's claim-ledger with CORROBORATES/CONTRADICTS pairs is the spec), investigation merge/move, and pin ergonomics (targets, toast+undo, stable rows). Add Ines's actionable chips on dossier self-critiques — drop receipt / flag label — because every click there is a gold label for the engine.
**Why second:** the dossier is unanimously the product's best artifact, and it's starved by capture. Carolina measured the distance precisely: "roughly 30 minutes of my own phone calls away from a filed story." Receipt pins + the claim table close most of that gap, and the verdict chips turn analyst work into the training data #204 needs — the flywheel, generated as a byproduct.

### Move 3 — Make the dossier publishable (engine/data work)
Three blockers, in order of leverage: **(a)** wire corroboration (SERP/Brave key or DOC 2.0 fix — possibly the highest leverage-per-effort item in the whole session; Marcos: "the single feature that would make the dossier publishable"), with pre-announce + cached-corpus degraded mode; **(b)** validate generated prose against its own measured tables before render (lens claims, shared-actor claims, "confirmed" in titles) — this also decides Diana's dossier-voice-upstream idea: it ships only behind this validator; **(c)** credibility tiers (#217) past "unknown 19 · wire 1," and fix the Brief staleness pipeline (ops) — honesty banners gain what/why/when fields as part of the same pass.

## Ruling (honest)

1. **The thesis is ALREADY the decided architecture** — R3 (2026-07-01, from
   Pedro's own earlier challenge): anchored-EMERGENT categories, the crisis-32
   demoted to seed anchors + an editorial lens; `category` open;
   `crisis_relevant` a flag, never a partition. Dynamic topics were always
   emergent (HDBSCAN, categories assigned AFTER via R3.1 typing =
   post-classification). The universe is the emergent field.
2. **The residue Pedro senses is real**: the atlas LEXICON lane
   (theme-hint-lex-v2) still pre-assigns signals into the 32 categories and
   still feeds surfaces. That is the un-executed half of R3 — **F4 is its
   death**, and this challenge RAISES F4's priority (already sequenced in the
   3-day route / post-gold engine session).
3. **NEW in this challenge — crisis as a DYNAMICS property, adopted**:
   - `crisis_relevant` (semantic harm potential, DeepSeek-typed) = one lens,
     KEEPS its name but is demoted from "the" crisis to "harm-potential lens".
   - **`crisis_dynamics` (new) = f(velocity, surprise, changed_10h)** from
     `topic_movement` — a measured STATE, recomputed continuously, no
     arbitrary human category. A slow-burn war is harm-relevant but
     dynamically calm; a 10× surging cultural story is dynamically hot.
     Surfaces (universe CRISIS chip, brief heating strip) should read the
     DYNAMICS lens by default and offer the harm lens as a filter — not
     conflate them.
   - Implementation: pure threshold spec over topic_movement (e.g. surprise
     ≥1.35 ∧ velocity>0.08 = the existing 'surging' trend label — already
     computed; serving change only). Small build, post-Day-2.
4. **Paper 1**: the 41.6%→48-54% lineage is the HISTORICAL baseline of the
   deprecated pre-split lane — documented as such (PR3.2 already re-scoped
   the question to anchored precision + open-set→P8). The go-forward headline
   metric = **lens-membership precision on emergent stories** (typing/gate
   quality), which is frame-independent. No number is defended against the
   architecture; the paper follows the build, not vice versa.
5. **Tonight's gold is NOT invalidated**: blitz labels are lens-membership
   truth ("substantively about election-legitimacy disputes?") — identical
   under pre-filter or post-classification frames. They transfer to F4's
   world as typing/gate gold.
